# SEO and content

How the site gets discovered, and how to write in the Kitchen's Worth voice.

## Meta pattern (per page)

`app/layout.tsx` sets the site-wide defaults:

```
title:        Kitchen's Worth — Abuja QSR & Party Packs. ...eat well & spend less.
description:  Fast, generous portions of jollof, small chops, party packs and
              platters delivered across Abuja. Card, transfer or bank. VAT
              included. WhatsApp to order.
```

Every page overrides `title` and `description` via `export const metadata`. Pattern:

| Page | Title | Description focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | `Kitchen's Worth — Abuja's quick-service kitchen. Party packs, platters, small chops.` | Hero value prop, delivery scope |
| `/menu` | `Full menu — Kitchen's Worth Abuja` | Category list, VAT-inclusive mention |
| `/menu/[slug]` | `<Item name> — Kitchen's Worth` | Item description, price, category context |
| `/party-packs` | `Party packs for any gathering — Kitchen's Worth Abuja` | Serves, contents, ordering window |
| `/bulk` | `Bulk orders — up to 46.4% off at 50+` | Tiered discount hook |
| `/catering` | `Catering for events across Abuja` | Event catering, WhatsApp enquiry |
| `/zones` | `Delivery areas and fees` | Zones, ETAs, multi-city note |
| `/track` | `Track my order` | Utility |
| `/about` | `Our story` | Brand voice |
| `/contact` | `Contact us` | Phone, WhatsApp, Instagram |

Titles are 55 chars or less so they don't truncate in Google. Descriptions are 155 chars or less.

## Structured data (JSON-LD)

Every public page emits `schema.org` JSON-LD in a `<script type="application/ld+json">`:

- **Site-wide**: `WebSite` (with `potentialAction: SearchAction` — even though we don't have a search page yet, declaring it helps Google surface a sitelinks search box later).
- **Home**: `Restaurant` schema — name, servesCuisine (`["Nigerian", "Continental"]`), address (Abuja), telephone (`+234 703 060 6021`), priceRange (`₦₦`), sameAs (`instagram.com/Kitchensworth.ng`), servesDelivery (`true`).
- **`/menu`**: `Menu` with a nested `hasMenuSection` for every category, each listing its items.
- **`/menu/[slug]`**: `MenuItem` with `name`, `description`, `image`, `suitableForDiet` (Vegan badge), `spicyLevel` (Spicy badge), `offers` (price in NGN).
- **`/party-packs`**: `Menu` + `MenuItem` for each pack.
- **`/catering`**: `FoodEstablishment` with an `potentialAction: OrderAction` pointing at the WhatsApp link.
- **`/track`**: no JSON-LD — private page.

The JSON-LD blocks are authored in `app/menu/[slug]/page.tsx` (see the `structuredData` variable). Any new page should follow the same pattern.

## Sitemap + robots

- `/sitemap.xml` is generated by `app/sitemap.ts`. Every public, indexable page is listed with a `lastModified` timestamp pulled from the DB.
- `/robots.txt` is generated by `app/robots.ts`. Points at the sitemap, disallows `/admin/*` and `/api/*`, sets a crawl delay of 0 (default).

Both are served as static routes; Vercel caches them for an hour.

## Open Graph and Twitter cards

Every page's metadata exports `openGraph` and `twitter`:

```ts
openGraph: {
  title, description,
  images: [{ url: '/og/menu.png', width: 1200, height: 630, alt }],
  type: 'website',
  siteName: "Kitchen's Worth",
  locale: 'en_NG',
}
```

Static OG images live in `public/og/` (one per main section). Item pages use the item's own photo as the OG image (via `og_image_url` on `menu_items`).

## Performance / Core Web Vitals

- The home page ships ~40 KB of JS (mostly the dock + cart context). Everything else is server-rendered HTML.
- Menu images use `next/image` with `sizes="(max-width: 640px) 100vw, 320px"` and `priority={i < 6}` for the first six cards.
- Fonts are `next/font/google` with `display: 'swap'` — Inter for UI, and a display font for the logo lockup.
- Vercel edge caching means TTFB on `/menu` is < 50 ms across West Africa.
- Lighthouse targets (staging): Performance ≥ 90, SEO ≥ 95, Accessibility ≥ 95, Best Practices ≥ 95.

## Brand voice

**"eat well & spend less."**

Everything else flows from that. The voice is warm, direct, generous, a little playful, never fussy. Sentences are short. We say "you," not "our customers."

### Do

- Lead with what the customer gets, not what we do: *"Generous jollof, delivered in 45 minutes"* — not *"We cook jollof and deliver it."*
- Use ₦ without decimals (a ₦1,500 price reads better than ₦1,500.00).
- Name Abuja districts naturally: *"from Wuse to Kubwa"*, not *"from Zone 4 to Zone 1"*.
- Reference food by mouthfeel and occasion: *"the kind of jollof that ends arguments"* — not *"premium aromatic basmati-blend jollof"*.
- Keep the tagline lower-case with the ellipsis exactly as-is: `...eat well & spend less.`

### Don't

- No corporate filler ("we're excited to announce", "at Kitchen's Worth, we believe").
- No fake scarcity ("limited stock!" when stock is unlimited).
- No emojis in H1s or body copy — only in WhatsApp order cards where they're functional.
- No price anchoring ("was ₦X, now ₦Y") unless the client actually changes prices.
- No "cheap." The tagline says *less*, not *cheap*. Cheap implies low quality. We're generous portions at fair prices.

### Words in the kitchen

Approved for menu descriptions: smoky, well-seasoned, generous, family-style, fresh-ground, hand-cut, slow-cooked, one-plate.

Banned: artisanal, curated, elevated, gourmet, premium, curated, sourced-from-farmers (unless literally true).

### Photography brief

Any photo uploaded through the admin should:

- Fill the frame with the food, not the plate's rim.
- Have visible steam, a hand about to grab, or a spoon mid-scoop — motion, not still-life.
- Not be shot straight down (overhead); shoot from 45° so the top and side of the dish are both visible.
- Avoid heavy filters. Natural light, slight warmth.

## Local SEO

- Register the business in Google Business Profile under Abuja. Categories: *Fast food restaurant*, *Catering service*.
- Link to the site from Instagram `@Kitchensworth.ng` profile.
- Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across GBP, Instagram, and the footer: `Kitchen's Worth · Wuse II, Abuja · +234 703 060 6021`.
